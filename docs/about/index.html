About
me
Hi, I’m Viktor — an AI engineer, computational biologist and founder. I work across statistical research, production software and product development, with a background in applied mathematics and a PhD in Biostatistics and Bioinformatics. I’m interested in tools that help people make sense of evidence, advance science and solve useful problems.

My journey

2011–2015

I studied Applied Mathematics and Computer Science at South Ural State University. Early software roles included game-server development, stock-photo search and document-image processing.

Looking for useful applications of mathematics led me towards computational biology.

2015–2017

During my master’s at St. Petersburg Polytechnic University, I studied machine learning and statistics at the Computer Science Center and worked on pharmaceutical and genomics algorithms at EPAM Systems.

Research with Peter Kharchenko’s lab at Harvard Medical School led to dropEst, a pipeline for accurate molecule counting in single-cell RNA sequencing.

2018–2021

I led data-science projects at Cleverbots in 2018–2019, working on forecasting, recommendations and production-process modelling.

From 2019 to 2021, I completed a PhD in Biostatistics and Bioinformatics at the University of Copenhagen with Konstantin Khodosevich and Peter Kharchenko, combining work with experimental biologists and computational researchers at Harvard Medical School.

2021–2024

I began independent AI and biotech consulting in September 2021. Through SEADS, I worked with non-profits on speech recognition, survey analysis and impact evaluation.

As CTO of Tentakel from 2022 to 2024, I hired and managed three developers and built LLM applications for querying business data and document collections.

2022–2025

At Altos Labs, I analysed single-cell and spatial data for cell-therapy development and benchmarked the effects of cell-segmentation errors. This work led to a co-first-author Nature Genetics paper in 2026.

Alongside consulting, I continued developing research software and received the SIB Bioinformatics Award’s PhD Paper Award in 2023.

2025–2026

I founded and built LabMap, a search engine and map of academic labs, handling the product from LLM data pipelines to its interface and promotion.

In 2026, I led production AI work for a German industrial manufacturer, building document workflows, board-meeting intelligence and multilingual news monitoring. I also explored a bio-waste venture through the Fifty Years 5050 accelerator and fieldwork in Ghana; that venture is paused.

Today my work brings together AI engineering, scientific methods and product development.
