Research & technicalcollaborations
  • AI for scientific discovery
    I build tools that help researchers find and interpret evidence: literature maps, academic-lab search and LLM-assisted analysis. Article Visual Search, LabMap and GPTCellAnnotator connect this interest to working software.
  • Single-cell & spatial biology
    My research covers Bayesian cell segmentation, integration of single-cell datasets and case-control analysis. I’m interested in collaborations on robust methods, reproducible benchmarks and tools that help experimental biologists interpret their data.
  • Reliable AI & decision support
    My interests include AI safety, neural-network interpretability and evidence-based decisions. Recent work spans source-checked document intelligence, multilingual news analysis and model evaluation, alongside earlier graph-regularisation research at AI Safety Camp.

Projects for collaboration

  • Article Visual Search
    I independently built an interactive map of scientific literature from PubMed and Semantic Scholar, combining embeddings and clustering with optional LLM-generated cluster summaries. Developed intermittently from May 2023 to March 2026, it follows my earlier work on mapping document collections.
    Explore the application
  • Baysor & spatial transcriptomics
    As lead author and maintainer of Baysor, I develop Bayesian cell-segmentation methods and reproducible research software. My related work with Altos Labs examined how segmentation errors affect spatial analysis, leading to a co-first-author Nature Genetics paper in 2026.
    Code and documentation
  • GPTCellAnnotator
    Built an LLM-assisted workflow for single-cell cluster annotation in 2023–2024. GPT proposes candidate cell types and markers; Scanpy identifies cluster markers, and GPT assigns labels from that evidence.
    View the code
  • Modular neural networks
    With an AI Safety Camp team, explored loss-function regularisation to encourage modular structure in neural networks, demonstrating the approach on an MLP and word2vec.
    Show more ↓
    I extended Graph Spectral Regularization to impose arbitrary target graph structures and used it to organise word2vec neurons into five modules, enabling visual inspection of the resulting embeddings.
